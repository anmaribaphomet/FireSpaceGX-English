FireSpace GX

Flame in free fall. FireSpace GX is a research and analysis platform for fire safety in space. It brings together NASA's microgravity combustion experiments —ACME, FLEX, SAFFIRE, BASS and more— so you can explore them, compare materials, identify combustion patterns and use artificial intelligence to anticipate possible fire scenarios.

It is designed for the scientific community, graduate students and aerospace engineers who design the crew's living areas: space stations, spacecraft, lunar bases, Martian surfaces and pressurized vehicles. Our goal: turn complex data into clear answers and make the next giant leap a safer one.

How to use it

What can you do here?

Three connected tools that take you from the question to the data, and from the data to physical intuition.

{{ mascota() }}

Ask ApiGx

Write in natural language, for example: "tell me how methanol flames behave". Pick a mode with the filter button: Ask, Research or Results. ApiGx answers and suggests which research to explore next.

Value: you find the relevant experiments in seconds without reading hundreds of reports.

Explore the live dashboard

While you chat, ApiGx builds a dashboard with the data it gathers. Filter fuels, move the amount and the O₂ concentration and watch when a chart crosses the risk threshold. Expand the panel with the ‹ arrow to see it in full.

Value: you spot patterns and risk peaks across experiments, compounds and gravity levels.

Play with the flame in 3D

In 3GX you pick a filter (conditions, PSI-68 compounds or mission environment) and sliders appear. For example, ethanol at 0.5: the flame changes its shape and color, and ApiGx uses NASA research to explain whether that flame is dangerous, why, and what to do.

Value: you feed predictive flammability models with immediate visual intuition.
